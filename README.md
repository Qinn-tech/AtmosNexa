# AtmosNexa

Malaysia's Air Quality, Weather & Environmental Monitoring Platform

AtmosNexa aims to make environmental information across Malaysia easier to access, understand, and explore.

## 🌏 Our Mission

To provide a clear and accessible platform for monitoring air quality, weather conditions, and environmental changes throughout Malaysia.

## 📊 Key Features

- Interactive Map — Explore air quality and environmental conditions across Malaysia.
- Air Quality Monitoring — Display Malaysia's Air Pollution Index (API/IPU), PM2.5, PM10, CO, NO₂, SO₂, and O₃.
- Weather Information — View temperature, humidity, wind, and rainfall data.
- Historical Trends — Explore historical measurements and environmental trends.
- Alerts — Help users stay informed about potentially unhealthy air quality.
- Estimated Data — Provide clearly labelled estimates for areas without direct monitoring data, when reliable modelling is available.

## 🇲🇾 Initial Coverage

Malaysia, with the long-term goal of expanding coverage from national and state levels to towns and local areas.

## 🛠️ Development

AtmosNexa is under development. Features and data coverage will expand progressively.

## ⚠️ Data Transparency

AtmosNexa aims to distinguish measured observations from estimated or modelled values. Data sources, timestamps, and limitations should be clearly identified wherever possible.
